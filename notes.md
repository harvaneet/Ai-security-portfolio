# Why is a long password usually better than a complex short one?

1. A longer password is better because it creates far more possible combinations, so an attacker has to guess many more options before finding the right one.

2. A short but complex password may look strong, but it still has a much smaller search space and can be cracked faster with brute-force or dictionary attacks. 

3. Longer passwords increase the number of possible guesses exponentially, making automated attacks much slower and less likely to succeed.
